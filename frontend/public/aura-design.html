I'd use a Redis-backed distributed lock with a TTL to prevent race conditions. The lock key would be derived from the resource ID, and I'd implement retry logic with exponential backoff for contention.

Good — you mentioned Redis locks with TTL. What happens if the lock holder crashes before releasing? Walk me through the failure scenario and your mitigation.
Candidate
If the holder crashes, the TTL ensures eventual release. But during that window, other clients are blocked. I'd mitigate with a watchdog thread that extends the lock if the operation is still running, and use fencing tokens to prevent stale writes.

Fencing tokens — solid. Now, your system needs to handle 10K writes/sec to a single hot key. Redis single-shard bottleneck. How do you redesign?
